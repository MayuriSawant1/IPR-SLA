Video Topic: Role of International Organizations (WIPO & WTO) in IPR Protection
Learning Process (250–300 words):


To prepare this video, I began by revisiting my article on WIPO and WTO, using it as the conceptual backbone for the video's structure. I identified the key segments I wanted to cover: the historical origins of WIPO and WTO, the landmark treaties they administer (particularly PCT and TRIPS), their collaborative relationship, and their relevance to developing countries like India.
For research, I referred to the official websites of WIPO (wipo.int) and the WTO (wto.org), supplemented by NPTEL lecture notes and academic resources on international trade law and IPR. I also reviewed publicly available case studies on TRIPS flexibilities used during the COVID-19 pandemic to make the content contemporary and relatable.
I prepared a structured script divided into six segments: Introduction, Role of WIPO, Role of WTO, TRIPS Agreement Deep-Dive, India's Position, and Conclusion. The script was written in simple, conversational language to make it accessible to a general audience while maintaining academic accuracy.
For the video creation, I used Google Slides to design clean and visually consistent presentation slides with key points, diagrams showing the relationship between WIPO and WTO, and a timeline of landmark treaties. I recorded the video using OBS Studio (free and open-source), with my slide presentation as the primary visual. The final video runs approximately 6 minutes and covers all key aspects of the topic in a structured and engaging manner.
The experience helped me develop research skills, communication and presentation skills, and the ability to simplify complex legal and economic concepts for a wider audience — all of which are valuable for both academic and professional settings.
